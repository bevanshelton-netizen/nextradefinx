# NexLearn — Complete Trading Curriculum

## Level 1 — Foundations
- What markets are and why prices move
- Shares, ETFs, indices, forex, commodities, CFDs and crypto
- Exchanges, brokers, liquidity and spreads
- Market orders, limit orders and stop orders
- Bid/ask, slippage and execution

## Level 2 — Reading Markets
- Candlesticks and price action
- Support and resistance
- Trends, ranges and breakouts
- Volume and volatility
- Moving averages and ATR
- Multi-timeframe analysis

## Level 3 — Professional Risk Management
- Position sizing
- Risk per trade
- Stop-loss design
- Reward/risk
- Daily loss limits
- Drawdown and exposure
- Kill switches
- Why protecting capital comes before making money

## Level 4 — Strategy Development
- Turning an idea into explicit rules
- Backtesting
- Out-of-sample testing
- Walk-forward testing
- Avoiding overfitting
- Measuring expectancy, win rate, profit factor and drawdown

## Level 5 — Professional Trader Behaviour
- Trading plans
- Journaling
- Psychology and discipline
- Avoiding revenge trading and overtrading
- Understanding news and event risk
- Knowing when not to trade

## Level 6 — Learning From Experienced Traders
- How professional traders form a thesis
- How to evaluate a trader's track record
- Copy-trading mechanics and risks
- Piggybacking as education, not blind copying
- Verifying risk-adjusted performance
- Recognising scams and unrealistic return claims

## Level 7 — Algorithmic Trading
- Rule-based strategies
- Signals and indicators
- Execution engines
- Broker APIs and Expert Advisors
- Paper trading
- Monitoring and alerts
- Fail-safe design
- Credential security

## Level 8 — Funded Trading
- Prop-firm evaluation models
- Simulated versus live funded accounts
- Drawdown rules
- Profit targets
- Consistency requirements
- Automation restrictions
- Jurisdiction and regulatory checks
- Never paying a challenge fee without informed approval

## Graduation
A learner graduates only when the system records sufficient evidence of knowledge, risk discipline and consistent simulated execution. Graduation does not guarantee funding or profits. It authorises a funding-provider review, not automatic live trading.

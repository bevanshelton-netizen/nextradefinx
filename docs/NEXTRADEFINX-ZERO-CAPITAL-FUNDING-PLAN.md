# NexTradeFinX Zero-Capital Funding Plan

## Objective
Build a path for a trader with no personal trading capital to learn, demonstrate competence, and pursue legitimate third-party trading capital without promising returns.

## Operating model
1. NexLearn — complete trading education from beginner through risk management, market structure, execution, psychology, and professional workflows.
2. NexSim — virtual capital and realistic market simulation.
3. NexAI Coach — evaluates process, risk discipline, and decision quality rather than rewarding reckless P&L.
4. NexScore — competency and consistency record.
5. NexAI Night Trader — paper-first algorithmic strategy with hard risk controls.
6. Funding Gate — only after evidence of competence, identify legitimate zero-cost/no-upfront-capital opportunities, competitions, sponsorships, or funded pathways.
7. Live Gate — actual live execution requires a permitted broker/funding arrangement, verified terms, jurisdictional eligibility, secure credentials, and explicit owner authorization.

## Rules
- Never promise or guarantee trading profits.
- Never represent simulated prop capital as guaranteed real cash.
- Never pay a challenge fee without explicit owner approval.
- Never request or commit broker credentials in source code.
- Keep live execution disabled until the funding provider's automation rules and account type are verified.
- Preserve a kill switch, per-trade risk cap, daily loss cap, and maximum exposure limits.

## Funding-watch criteria
Prefer opportunities that:
- require no upfront trading capital or challenge fee;
- are open to South African applicants where legally available;
- provide transparent terms and current eligibility;
- permit algorithmic trading when relevant;
- clearly distinguish simulated evaluation from live funded execution;
- have reputable, verifiable operators.

Reject or flag opportunities involving guaranteed returns, unclear counterparties, hidden fees, pressure to deposit, unverifiable capital claims, or terms that conflict with the user's jurisdiction.

## Current technical status
NexAI Night Trader is paper-first. Live execution is disabled. Broker adapters must be permissioned and credentials must remain outside GitHub.

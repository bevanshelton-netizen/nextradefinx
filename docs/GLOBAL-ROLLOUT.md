# Global Rollout Map

## Wave 0 — Controlled beta
- NexAI multilingual market assistant
- NexLearn
- NexAI Market Lab
- Practice Trade
- Prediction Ledger
- NexRisk
- No live orders

## Wave 1 — South Africa
Languages: English + South African language set.
Markets: JSE market intelligence first, then broker-connected execution after approvals.
Goal: prove safety, support, onboarding and conversion.

## Wave 2 — Pan-African
Prioritise English, French, Arabic, Portuguese, Swahili, Hausa, Nigerian Pidgin, Amharic, Yoruba and Igbo.
Localise currencies, examples, exchanges and regulation by country.

## Wave 3 — India, Southeast Asia & Middle East
Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Indonesian and Arabic.
Use local regulated execution partners. Build local exchange/data adapters.

## Wave 4 — Latin America
Spanish + Portuguese.
Local broker integrations and region-specific education.

## Wave 5 — East Asia
Mandarin Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
Launch research/education wherever execution permissions are not yet available; add execution only through compliant local partners.

## Wave 6 — Europe & North America
English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and other demand-led locales.
Connect through regulated broker/dealer and clearing partners.

## Global discovery
Each locale gets:
- native-language landing page;
- search-optimised education library;
- local-market watchlists;
- local currency and timezone;
- country-specific risk/regulatory disclosures;
- locally relevant onboarding examples;
- app-store/local social acquisition campaigns.

The global product remains one NexAI identity with region-specific market access.

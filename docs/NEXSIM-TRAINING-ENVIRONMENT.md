# NexSim Training Environment

NexSim gives each learner a virtual trading account so they can experience market decisions without risking personal capital.

## Default training account
- Starting virtual equity: $100,000
- Maximum one open position
- Default maximum daily loss: 2%
- Full trade journal
- Equity and drawdown tracking
- Stop-loss and target support
- Kill switch when daily loss limit is reached

## Training loop
1. Learn a concept in NexLearn.
2. Receive a market scenario.
3. Make the trade decision.
4. NexSim records execution and outcome.
5. NexAI Coach evaluates risk, timing, thesis and discipline.
6. NexScore updates competency.
7. Repeat until the learner demonstrates consistent process.

## Important boundary
NexSim is simulated training capital. It does not represent a promise of funding or profits. Passing NexSim does not automatically create a live account. Live execution remains separately gated behind broker/funding-provider eligibility, jurisdictional requirements, account rules, secure credentials and explicit owner authorization.

# NexAI Coach + NexScore + Funding Readiness

## Purpose

NexTradeFinX now evaluates the trader's process, not just profit.

The progression is:

`NexLearn → NexSim → NexAI Coach → NexScore → Funding Review → Live Gate`

## NexAI Coach

Coach feedback checks:

- planned versus actual risk
- stop usage
- target/exit discipline
- plan adherence
- revenge trading
- overtrading
- drawdown
- whether a loss was still a disciplined trade

The coach produces a process score, risk score, discipline score, grade, lessons and a next action.

## NexScore

NexScore combines:

- market knowledge: 20%
- risk discipline: 25%
- consistency: 20%
- trading process: 15%
- drawdown control: 10%
- simulated trade history: 5%
- profitable-day history: 5%

Tiers:

- `FOUNDATION`
- `DEVELOPING`
- `COMPETENT`
- `FUNDING_READY`

`FUNDING_READY` also requires at least 50 simulated trades, 20 profitable days and maximum drawdown of 10% or less.

## Funding gate

The existing funding gate independently requires:

- risk score ≥ 80
- consistency score ≥ 75
- at least 50 trades
- at least 20 profitable days
- drawdown ≤ 10%

Passing means **eligible for external-provider review**, not guaranteed funding.

## Live safety boundary

NexAI readiness can never enable live execution. Live trading remains disabled until an approved broker/provider, jurisdiction, account type, market-data permissions, automation rules, secure credentials and explicit owner authorization have been verified.

No performance result is a promise of future returns. Simulated capital is not real capital.

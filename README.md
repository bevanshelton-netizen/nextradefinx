# IZAKHONO INVEST™

MVP of the IZAKHONO AI financial-intelligence and paper/simulation platform.

## Included
- AI Market Brain dashboard
- Opportunity scoring and WHY explanations
- Portfolio Intelligence
- Scenario Lab / stress simulation
- FORTRESS security boundary
- Human decision gate
- Simulation-only mode; no customer funds or live trade execution
- IZAKHONO ONE AI Client Acquisition Engine preview at `/acquisition`

## IZAKHONO ONE commercial engine
The acquisition surface establishes a reusable agent workflow for future IZAKHONO businesses:

`ICP → Research → Company Discovery → Decision Makers → Verification → Outreach Draft → Human Approval → Meeting → Follow-up → CRM`

The current surface is a safe prototype. It does not send unsolicited messages, purchase domains, access private contact data, or make commitments on behalf of a customer without the required permissions and approval controls.

## Deployment boundary
Live execution, custody, regulated investment services, KYC/AML and payment settlement require the appropriate authorised partners and production controls. This repository contains no production secrets or credentials.

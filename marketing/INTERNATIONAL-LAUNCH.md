## Positioning
NexAI Global Markets is positioned publicly as an Africa-built multilingual financial education, market-intelligence, risk-learning and paper-trading platform.

Current paid-ad promise:
- Learn global markets in your language.
- View major market boards with honest real-time/delayed labels.
- Practise with paper trading before risking capital.
- Learn risk management before return.
- No profit guarantees.
- No real-money execution.
- Do not advertise trade signals, guaranteed returns, "get rich", or live brokerage execution.

The AI chat endpoint is deployed but model generation remains account-activation dependent. Do not make AI answering the primary paid-ad promise until production smoke testing passes.

## Compliance gate before paid activation
For each country:
- confirm platform financial-services ad classification;
- confirm whether advertiser verification is required;
- publish required business identity / address / fee disclosures before Google Ads where applicable;
- keep claims education-first;
- exclude complex speculative-product promotion unless separately eligible and certified;
- confirm privacy/cookie requirements;
- confirm age targeting and financial-ad restrictions.

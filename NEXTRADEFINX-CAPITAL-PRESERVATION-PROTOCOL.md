# NexTradeFinX Capital Preservation Protocol

**Status:** ACTIVE — controlled validation
**Owner:** IZAKHONO AFRICA (PTY) LTD
**Purpose:** Establish the operating rules for NexTradeFinX while the owner is under financial pressure and before any live-money trading is authorised.

## 1. Capital rule
No personal emergency funds, creditor-payment funds, borrowed money, business operating cash, or essential household funds may be committed to trading.

## 2. Execution rule
Live brokerage execution remains disabled until the strategy passes documented paper/shadow validation and all required broker, KYC, regulatory, security and operational controls are verified.

## 3. Validation rule
Every paper/shadow trade must record: timestamp, instrument, entry, exit, position size, stop, target, reason, model confidence, realised P/L, maximum adverse excursion, maximum favourable excursion and drawdown impact.

## 4. Performance gate
Promotion to any higher-risk mode requires a statistically meaningful validation period and acceptable risk metrics. A single profitable day, screenshot, simulated balance or advertised return is not evidence of profitability.

## 5. Kill switches
The system must support daily loss limits, maximum position exposure, maximum concurrent positions, strategy disablement and emergency shutdown before live execution is considered.

## 6. Revenue priority
While trading is being validated, NexTradeFinX should pursue non-custodial revenue opportunities such as subscriptions, education, simulation, analytics and trader tooling. No customer funds are to be held by NexTradeFinX unless the required regulatory structure and authorisations exist.

## 7. Truth standard
No user-facing claim may imply guaranteed returns, guaranteed income, certainty of prediction or risk-free trading. Simulated results must be clearly labelled simulated.

## 8. Deployment standard
Follow the IZAKHONO owned-first infrastructure directive. Preserve a verified external fallback until the owned route passes its public verification gates.

## 9. Emergency financial context
The owner's current financial pressure increases the need for capital preservation. It does not justify increasing trading risk. The system must therefore optimise first for survival, evidence and repeatability, then for scale.
